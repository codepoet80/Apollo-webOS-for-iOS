F.A.Q.

Is this an official app made by Pandora?
No, it was developed by Taylor Kimmett and Jacob Morrison based strictly off of Pandora's official webOS phone version.

Why does content take a while to load sometimes(song information, art, etc.)?
That's completely Pandora's servers, or your internet connection, not the application, however we will do the best that we can to optimize load times.

How do I delete a station?
Place your finger on the radio you want to delete and drag your finger across the screen, left or right, to bring up the 'Delete' dialog.

How do I add variety/rename a station?
Due to the fact that we are using the same Pandora API from the original phone application, we do not have access to these features. If we could get access or if Pandora were to update their API, be sure that we would add it.

How do I search for a genre?
Due to the fact that we are using the same Pandora API from the original phone application, we do not have access to these features. If we could get access or if Pandora were to update their API, be sure that we would add it.

Will my actions in the app be reflected on the Pandora website?
Yes, all song likes/dislikes, new radios, and bookmark information is stored on Pandora's servers, not locally.

Why can't we have unlimited skips?
Although it is very much possible for us to remove skips, we will not break Pandora's terms of service. Pandora makes it clear that the skip limit is so they don't have to pay big money for licensing songs that you only listen to for 10 seconds. If you visit the discussion below, other people have third-party ways of making skips unlimited on Apollo and much more.

v0.9.1 (Initial App Catalog release)

Welcome to "Apollo", a third-party Pandora Media services application built using the Enyo Javascript Framework.


Here are a few steps to make sure you get the most out of the application.
  • To start listening to a radio station, select a station on the left, or search for one using the search bar at the bottom left of the screen.
  • To gain access to settings or help, tap the button at the top right corner of the screen.
  • To dismiss the settings/help pane, place your finger on the grab button and swipe it to the right off the screen.
  • After you've listened to a song on a given station, you'll notice its album art gets placed in a scroller at the top of the screen. Try tapping on that album art to see more information.
  • To give feedback, go to the 'Help' section by tapping the button in the top right corner of the screen.
